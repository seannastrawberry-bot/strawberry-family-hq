About automatic replanning

The on-device engine shifts future activities after naps, outings and delays; keeps completed tasks untouched; and protects essential routines from easy-day mode. It does not know the weather, your calendar or this ChatGPT conversation. An AI connection would require a secure server and separate setup.

🔒 Your ticked tasks and notes are stored in this iPad's browser where supported. The hosted website itself is public, so do not add personal details to its source files. No AI service or online synchronisation is connected.
