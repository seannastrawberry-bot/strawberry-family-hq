                        Backups and personalisation

                        Phone & iPad sync

                        Day settings

                        Version 6.52 · Save selected Quick Capture reminders to both devices.

                        Choose Fixed appointment, Essential care, Normal or Optional when editing activities. Replanning does not move fixed appointments or essential care. Times stay on this iPad.

                        Daily history and recovery

                        Notes save as you type and are included in the daily archive. A day is archived when the date changes while Family HQ is running, or the next time you open it. The browser keeps up to 31 recent snapshots. Export them regularly to keep a copy in Files.

                        Local history is available when browser storage works.

                        Back up your planner

                        Copy the backup text to a safe place or paste a previous backup to restore. This is also a manual way to transfer between devices.

                        About automatic replanning

                        The on-device engine shifts future activities after naps, outings and delays; keeps completed tasks untouched; and protects essential routines from easy-day mode. Connected Google Calendar appointments are fixed when replanning. Weather is available at the top of Family HQ.

                        🔒 Your timetable and notes save in this browser. The hosted website itself is public, so do not add personal details to its source files. Google Calendar can provide appointments and drafts. Household jobs and optional repeating routines sync when connected.
